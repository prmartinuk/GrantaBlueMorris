---
title: Bug Hotel
tradition: Granta
type: 4 man handkerchief dance
summary: Square insects
step: 3 double steps, 2 plain capers, 2 double steps, cross backs, jump. Hankies up/down for figures, messing up hair for chorus.
formation: Set 
chorus: | 
    * 1st corners double step in, out, cross
    * All join in for plain capers
    * Double step two places clockwise around set
    * Repeat with 2nd couple
---
* Caterpillar Lines (all face up, lines passing R at top, face in for capers half way round, pass L at bottom, face in for cross backs)
* Ch
* Butterfly Hey (4 man vertical hey - hankies as wings)
* Ch
* Shield Bug (Tight set facing up, 2 double steps, cross back, jump to face right. Repeat 4 times. Wave outside hands only)
* Ch - insects disperse into crowd on final two double steps
