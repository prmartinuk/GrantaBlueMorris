---
title: Skirmish
tradition: Adderbury
type: 6 person stick dance
image:
summary: 
step: Double step x2, single step x2, jump
formation: 6 as set
chorus: | 
    * Clash forehand + backhand with partner
    * 1st corners step in - all clash fore + back with person they are close to
    * 2nd corners step in - all clash fore + back with person they are close to
    * Back to normal set -  all clash fore + back with partner
    * Repeat
---
* Foot up (dance up and back x2)
* Ch
* Half hands (across the set passing right shoulders, back, left shoulder, back)
* Ch
* Process down (top couple down, back, top two couples down, back)
* Ch
* Hands around (give right hands go round, pull back, repeat with left hands)
* Ch
* Process up (bottom couple down, back, bottom two couples down, back)
* Ch
* Hey (parallel along sides)
* Ch
