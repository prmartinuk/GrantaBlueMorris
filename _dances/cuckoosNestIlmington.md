---
title: Cuckoos Nest
tradition: Ilmington
type: 3 person stick dance
image: 
summary: Stick and stamp
step: Double step x2, step (long twist), galley - right foot start
formation: Triangle - point away from musician
chorus: | 
    * All clash high x3 + stamp right foot with clash
    * All clash low x3 + stamp left foot with clash
    * 1 + 2 clash tip + butt + hop right foot
    * 2 + 3 clash tip + butt + hop left foot
    * 3 + 1 clash tip + butt + hop right foot
    * All clash + jump
    * Half hey - 3 finish by reversing through middle to invert triangle
    * Repeat
