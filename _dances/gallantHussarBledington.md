---
title: Gallant Hussar
tradition: Bledington
type: 3 person handkerchief dance
image: 
summary: Hookleg into line
step: Double step x2, Hookleg, Plain Caper
formation: Triangle, point away from musician
chorus: | 
    * Cross (passing right shoulder) to opposite
      * 1st time side step
      * 2nd time spring capers
      * 3rd time fore capers
      * 4th time upright capers
    * Hookleg into line
    * Spring caper (left foot) to start half hey
    * Hookleg back into position
    * Capers in position
---
