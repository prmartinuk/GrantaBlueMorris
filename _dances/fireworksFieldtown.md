---
title: Fireworks
tradition: Fieldtown
type: 3 man stick dance
summary: Triangle throw and clash
step: 2 Double steps, 2 cross backs / galley, jump
formation: Line as if odd side of set
chorus: | 
    * Galley into triangle, jump - all clash
    * 1-2 Throw stick and clash
    * 2-3 Throw and clash
    * 3-1 Throw and clash
    * Jump to face out
    * 4 double steps (R foot start)
      * 1 out
      * 1 curve round left
      * 1 into middle
      * 1 crossing right shoulders - all clash on 1st beat 
    * Repeat in inverted triangle
    * Galley into line rotating the set 90 degrees anti-clockwise (except for Rounds)
